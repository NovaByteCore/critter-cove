CRITTER COVE WIDGET FOR ANDROID (KWGT)
======================================
Shows one tiny task and a critter line. Changes every hour, matched to the
time of day. Tapping it opens Critter Cove.

1. Install "KWGT Kustom Widget Maker" from the Play Store (free).
2. Long-press your home screen > Widgets > KWGT > pick the 4x2 size and drop it.
3. Tap the empty widget. KWGT opens. Tap "+" (top right) to add items:

   a) Add a TEXT item (the task)
      - Tap it > Text > tap the formula icon and paste EVERYTHING
        between the two lines marked TASK below.
      - Font size about 18, bold.

   b) Add a second TEXT item (the critter line)
      - Paste EVERYTHING between the two lines marked CRITTER below.
      - Font size about 13.

   c) Optional: add a third TEXT item that just says  🦝 Critter Cove
      and a rounded RECTANGLE behind everything as the background
      (Playful Cove cream: #FFF4E6, text: #1E2D38, green: #6FC277).

4. Make it open the app: tap the top-level layer (Root/Overlap group) >
   Touch > "+" > Action: Open Link > paste:
   https://novabytecore.github.io/critter-cove/

5. Tap Save (the disk icon). Done.

If a text item shows the formula itself, or is blank at one hour of the day,
tell Claude what you see and it will adjust the formula.
